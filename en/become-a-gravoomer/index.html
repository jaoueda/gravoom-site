How it works

From your first message to your first job

  1. You sign up

    Two minutes with the form above: your town, your area, your availability. No business status is needed at this stage.

  2. We get to know you

    A welcome call. If you have no status yet, we set up your micro-entreprise together, online, during the call. Then we send you our illustrated visit guide.

  3. A practice visit

    On a grave you already look after, a relative’s for example. You send us the before and after photos, and we check the cleaning and the framing together.

  4. Your jobs, paid

    Close to home, with every detail: the grave, where it is, the flowers. You send the photos, and you are paid for the visit.

FAQ

Questions we are often asked

Do I need a business status?

Not to sign up. To be paid legally in France, yes: a micro-entreprise, free to set up online on the official URSSAF website. We set it up with you during the welcome call, before your first job. Contributions are based on what you take in: no jobs in a month, nothing to pay.

I am retired or a student. Can I join?

Yes. You can be a micro-entrepreneur while retired or studying. What you earn adds to your other income: if you receive any benefits, check with the body that pays them.

What is the practice visit for?

To get started without pressure. You do it on a grave you already look after, with our guide, then send us the before and after photos. We check the cleaning and the framing together before your first job for a family.

What about insurance?

You need professional liability insurance (responsabilité civile professionnelle). We tell you what it must cover and where to find it easily.

Do I need equipment?

Very little: a soft brush, a sponge, a bucket, gloves and a bag for green waste. No harsh products.

How long does a visit take?

Usually between 45 minutes and an hour and a half, depending on the state of the grave.

Can I turn down a job?

Of course. You only accept what suits you, with no minimum.

Who buys the flowers?

You buy them from a florist near the cemetery and we reimburse you, or they are supplied to you directly. Every job spells it out.
