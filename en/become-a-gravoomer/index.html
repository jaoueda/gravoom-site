FAQ

Questions we are often asked

Do I need a business status?

Yes, to be paid legally in France. A micro-entreprise is free to set up online on the official URSSAF website. If you already have a company, that is enough. We guide you at every step.

What about insurance?

You need professional liability insurance (responsabilité civile professionnelle). We tell you what it must cover and where to find it easily.

Do I need equipment?

Very little: a soft brush, a sponge, a bucket, gloves and a bag for green waste. No harsh products.

How long does a visit take?

Usually between 45 minutes and an hour and a half, depending on the state of the grave.

Can I turn down a job?

Of course. You only accept what suits you, with no minimum.

Who buys the flowers?

You buy them from a florist near the cemetery and we reimburse you, or they are supplied to you directly. Every job spells it out.
