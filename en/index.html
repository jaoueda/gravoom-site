How it works

As simple as a favour between neighbours

  1. You get in touch

    An email or a WhatsApp message is enough: the name on the grave, the cemetery, what you would like and when. We reply to confirm everything.

  2. We visit the cemetery

    Someone who lives near the cemetery goes there in person. They gently clean the grave with clear water and lay the flowers you chose.

  3. You see the result

    You receive before and after photos, dated and located. Peace of mind, and a moment to share with the whole family.

FAQ

Questions we are often asked

What if I don’t remember exactly where the grave is?

That’s fine, it happens a lot. Drop a pin on the map where you think it is and tell us what you remember. If needed, we check the cemetery register at the town hall.

Where do you operate?

We are starting in the north of France: Douai, Somain, Aniche, Denain, Valenciennes and the towns around them. Elsewhere in France, tell us the town of the cemetery: we look for someone local and tell you honestly whether we can do it. You can book from anywhere in the world.

What if there is nobody near the cemetery yet?

We will tell you honestly and look for someone in the area. You pay nothing until the visit is confirmed.

Can I choose the flowers?

Of course. A yellow, bronze or purple chrysanthemum, or a seasonal bouquet: just tell us what they would have liked.

How do I pay?

You pay nothing when you send your request. Once the visit is confirmed, we send you a secure link to pay by card; a bank transfer is also possible. If the visit cannot take place after all, you are refunded in full. The details are in our terms of sale.

What if I’m not happy with the result?

Tell us within seven days of receiving the photos: we go back at no charge, or we refund you.

When will I get the photos?

On the day of the visit, by email or WhatsApp, with a note of what was done.
