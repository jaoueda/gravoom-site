Our terms, in plain words

These terms apply to every visit a private customer orders from Gravoom.

2. What we do

Gravoom arranges the cleaning of graves in France and the laying of flowers. The visit is carried out by a local person, an individual or an independent professional, whom we have chosen and who holds professional liability insurance.

  • Cleaning: the headstone, plaque and surround cleaned with clear water and a soft brush, withered flowers removed, before and after photos.
  • Cleaning and flowers: cleaning, plus a chrysanthemum or a seasonal bouquet.
  • Four seasons: four visits with flowers over twelve months, one each season.
  • Restoring visit: for a grave left untended for a long time, on a free quote.

We do not repair stone, restore inscriptions or move any part of the monument. If the grave needs it, we tell you and can point you to a stonemason.

3. Prices

The prices are those shown on the site on the day of your request. They are in euros, include all taxes, and cover travel, the visit and the photos. The price of a restoring visit is given to you as a quote before you order.

4. Your request and our confirmation

A request sent from the site, by email or on WhatsApp commits you to nothing. We first check that someone can go to the cemetery, then confirm in writing the service, its price and the planned date. The order is made when you accept that confirmation and pay.

By ordering, you state that you are a relative of the deceased or are acting with the family’s agreement.

5. Payment

You pay nothing before our confirmation. You then pay by card through a secure payment link, or by bank transfer. The Four seasons plan is paid once for the four visits. It ends after twelve months and is never renewed without your agreement.

6. The visit

The visit takes place on the agreed date or, if no date was set, within thirty days of your payment. The before and after photos are sent to you the same day, by email or on WhatsApp.

If the weather, a closed cemetery or another serious obstacle forces us to move the visit, we tell you and agree a new date. If we cannot carry out the visit after all, you are refunded in full.

7. Your right to withdraw

You have fourteen days from the day the order is made to change your mind, without giving a reason. Write to us at info@gravoom.com, or use the form at the bottom of this page. We refund you within fourteen days, by the same means of payment.

If you want the visit to take place before those fourteen days are over, you ask us expressly when you order. In that case:

  • if you withdraw when part of the visit has already been carried out, you pay for the part already done;
  • once the visit has been fully carried out at your request, the right to withdraw can no longer be used for that visit, which you acknowledge when you order.

For the Four seasons plan, if you withdraw, each visit already carried out is due at a quarter of the price of the plan.

8. Cancelling or moving a visit

In addition to the right to withdraw, you can cancel or move a visit at no charge up to forty-eight hours before the planned date. After that, the visit can be moved once at no charge.

You can end the Four seasons plan at any time: visits not yet carried out are refunded, at a quarter of the price of the plan per visit.

9. Our commitment

If you are not happy with the result, tell us within seven days of receiving the photos. We go back at no charge or, if you prefer, refund the visit. This commitment is in addition to your legal rights.

10. Our liability

We work gently: clear water and a soft brush, never a pressure washer or acidic products. The photos taken before the visit show the state of the grave when we arrive. We are liable for damage caused by our work, but not for wear, cracks or damage that was already there, nor for what happens to flowers and objects after our visit.

11. The photos

The photos are for you. We do not publish them without your agreement; if you allow us to show one, the names are hidden.

12. Your personal data

We use your contact details, the name on the grave and its location only to arrange the visit, send you the photos and keep our accounts. The name and location are passed to the person who goes to the cemetery. Your data is kept by Advaltis Limited, in the United Kingdom, for as long as these purposes and our legal obligations require, and is never sold.

You can ask to see, correct or erase your data by writing to info@gravoom.com, and you can complain to the French data protection authority (CNIL) if you believe your rights are not respected.

13. Complaints and governing law

For any complaint, write to us at info@gravoom.com. We reply within ten working days. These terms are governed by French law. In case of a dispute, you can bring it before the court of your place of residence.
